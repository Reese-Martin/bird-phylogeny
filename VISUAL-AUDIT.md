# Bird presentation and overlap audit

Reviewed 19 November 2026. This audit covers the radial tree, birds-only presentations, MDS map, controls, selection, illustration sizing and annotation layers. It separates implemented corrections from future design work. The [performance audit](<PERFORMANCE-AUDIT.md>) documents computational costs and a ranked engineering backlog.

## Presentation rules

- The visible phylogenetic tree uses portrait-footprint angular spacing while retaining published topology, source-tip sequence and True-distance dated radii. Larger portraits receive more room, proportional to tile area raised to 0.625; internal branch angles follow weighted descendant intervals. Portraits retain that displayed tip angle and can move outward to make room. Three deterministic packing passes choose the smallest maximum radius without resizing or hiding birds; mean radius can increase. The minimum-width/count-blend draft was not selected.
- With branches hidden, the phylogenetic bird circle closes unused angular gaps while preserving source-tip sequence. Both birds-only styles allocate more angular room to larger portrait footprints, proportional to tile area raised to 0.625, without changing image dimensions. A provisional 0.36-radius center grows to 12% of that provisional maximum extent when needed, reserving count space for large collections. Angles and radii are decorative; showing the tree restores its footprint-spaced branch presentation and selected radial branch-distance mode.
- Color ordering is an explicit, separate **birds-only** arrangement. It preserves order-group sequence and sorts illustrations inside each order. Its angles and radii have no evolutionary-distance interpretation. It never changes the MDS fit.
- Recorded membership is not sampled to make room. Source-tip lumps can repeat an illustration in the phylogenetic view; placement gaps remain explicit, and color view has one portrait per species including birds without source placement.
- Everybird's original mass, painted-area correction and relative size modes remain intact. Collision geometry is a separate concern.
- MDS taxonomic selection changes emphasis only: members keep their colors, nonmembers remain visible in gray, and no group boxes are painted. Nonmembers stay gray during hover or individual selection; layout, packing and camera stay fixed.
- Bird map uses a fixed common sticker scale without a size slider. **Averaged size / True size** remains available for relative body sizes.
- Image resolution follows displayed physical size across 32/64/128/384 px. Changing tier never changes bird size or placement; a ready image remains visible while its replacement loads.
- Light/Dark appearance changes page and canvas palettes without changing geometry, camera or original illustration colors. System is the default, the header button makes an explicit choice, and **Use system theme** restores OS tracking. PNG scenes and footers use the selected palette.
- Aves, branch-length mode, root age and the calibrated distance scale occupy the right-aligned footer outside the plot. Ring-date captions and the center badge no longer mask branches. PNG exports reserve their own footer row; dated rings remain visible in True distance.
- Labels, selection decoration and interface controls must be included in an overlap review; zero bird-packing overlaps alone do not validate the complete rendered scene.

Details flow below the plot on narrow screens. [ExplorerControls.css](<src/components/ExplorerControls.css>) reserves actual layout space. |

| Equal hidden slots still produced long arms and a tiny count for 700 true-size birds | Large and small portraits received the same angular allocation, while a fixed small center made count text hard to read | A tuned tile-area exponent of 0.625 gives large portraits more angular room, and an adaptive center reserves count space. | The Bird map retained a common-size slider after full packing became the only presentation | Another tuning control added visual weight and triggered extra repacking | The map now fixes common scale at 1 and keeps the two relative body-size choices. The redundant slider panel is removed. |
| The MDS count badge and caption shared drawing space with birds | Pan/zoom could move artwork under the badge | A protected world-space center slot now contains the species count and optional highlighted total. | Area-averaged sizing masks can lose very thin native image features | A coarse zero-overlap result did not prove that every fine feature was clear at every zoom | A separate conservative collision mask now unions native 32/64/128/384 px alpha footprints with a one-source-pixel guard per tier. Original sizing/hit masks and source artwork are unchanged. Missing collision metadata uses full rectangular occupancy. |
| Full overviews decoded larger images than their display size required | A complete collection could use unnecessary decoded memory or reload when changing resolution | Small 32/64 px transparent tiers supplement 128/384 px; DPR is capped at 2, hysteresis avoids repeated tier switching, and a ready fallback stays visible until replacement. | Reloading recomputed an unchanged completed map | Repeat visits waited for fitting and packing again | A validated persistent fit/layout cache reuses completed coordinates and geometry without changing their arrangement. | The interface and canvas drawing were fixed to light colors | Night viewing had no coherent alternative, and styling only the page would leave canvas/export backgrounds mismatched | System/Light/Dark resolution, a header toggle, prepaint initialization and shared CSS/canvas palettes cover controls, world map, graph annotations and PNG footers. The [radial renderer](<src/tree/renderer.ts>), [radial geometry/annotation helpers](<src/tree/radial-sticker-placement.ts>) and [MDS renderer](<src/components/ClusterCanvas.tsx>) share these geometric protections. They do not hide species to create room. Panning still moves birds outside the viewport in the ordinary way.

## Color presentation

The color capture covers all **11,027** bundled illustrations. It samples opaque interior pixels, excluding faint edges/shadows and eroding the pale sticker border. It measures a predominant area-based color family and representative OKLab cluster, with deterministic neutral/lightness and hue ordering. The sequence is black, gray, white, brown, then warm-to-cool color families; missing artwork is placed last.

This is a measurement of an illustration, not an authoritative species, sex or seasonal plumage trait. A large pale breast may dominate even when a bird is recognized by a small bright patch. Color arrangement remains optional for that reason. See [COLOR-NOTES.md](<COLOR-NOTES.md>) for thresholds, source pins, examples, coverage and repeated capture hashes.

## Validation and its limits

- Pure geometry tests cover whitespace label placement, exclusion circles, leader clipping, viewport fits, tiny-scale markers, native-aspect containment, repeated source concepts and 700-portrait layouts.
- Earlier exact-global-angle geometry checks retained all 700 portraits and plotted rays with zero independently checked tile overlaps. Those checks predate the accepted footprint-spaced visible tree and do not establish unchanged global angles in the current view.
- Current weighted-tree geometry independently retains all 193 Regional benchmark and 700 combined portraits, source-tip order and original dimensions, with zero pairwise tile intersections and zero True-distance dated-radius error in both relative size modes. Regional benchmark's True-size Pelecaniformes wedge is **41.7117°**, with maximum portrait radius **2.6568**, closely matching the pure-footprint draft. Full-circle weighting is independent of taxonomy/background visibility; a stable explicit angular domain supports fans. Shared source tips retain one angular anchor, and tiny positive background slots keep the complete source order representable.
- Earlier 700-species color arrangements and 316-species color fans retained one portrait per species, color order and zero tile overlaps in both size modes. Hidden presentations now use a compact circle, including for a selected group; those earlier fan measurements are historical.
- The initial radial spacing experiment independently checked 24 layout combinations with zero rectangle overlaps and unchanged dimensions. Regional benchmark's 193-species visible True-distance/True-size maximum decorative radius fell from **6.4882 to 5.4125**; all 700 fell from **15.4162 to 12.2396**. That run's hidden equal-slot, fixed-0.36 circles measured **3.0605 / 6.8939** respectively in True size. Those hidden values are historical experiments, preceding footprint weighting and the adaptive count center. They are geometric extents, not branch dates or a whole-scene browser-pixel overlap proof.
- Final footprint-weighted/adaptive hidden circles retained every portrait, dimensions and sequence with zero independently checked rectangle overlaps in all four country/size fixtures. Regional benchmark's maximum radius was **1.8154 averaged / 2.3636 true size**, with center radius **0.36**. All 700 measured **3.5171 / 4.6632**, with centers **0.4108 / 0.5515**. These display-geometry results are in ignored `test-results/radial-weighted-slots.json`; count legibility and annotation behavior require the separate browser checks.
- The earlier independent MDS native-alpha sweep covered 128/384 px, both size modes and 20/100% common size, finding zero nonzero-alpha intersections in those eight configurations with the initial conservative masks. Those historical layout measurements are distinct from the current four-tier capture.
- Current capture checks all 44,108 native tier masks against the shared four-tier collision grid for 11,027 species. All 22,054 overview derivatives retain transparency and nonempty alpha, preserve full-canvas aspect within rounding and repeat byte-identically. This verifies source coverage and image transforms, not every possible browser interpolation or annotation state.
- Browser verification separately checks artwork settlement, control/card separation, annotation behavior, taxonomy highlighting, mode switching, export and narrow layouts. Private screenshots and diagnostics remain under ignored `personal/` and `test-results/`.
- Focused theme tests cover saved/System preference, OS and cross-tab changes, unavailable storage, prepaint initialization and text/accent contrast. Renderer tests check identical placement and hit targets under both palettes. Theme changes must redraw without refitting, repacking or moving the camera; browser observations are recorded separately rather than inferred from palette tests.

These checks do not guarantee every possible viewport, user pan, future illustration or browser rendering edge case. A clipped bird at a viewport edge is different from an image hidden by a floating interface panel. Desktop narrow-screen testing does not establish real phone speed or real touch ergonomics.

Image-cache metrics are native RGBA/canvas estimates, including gray variants, rather than total browser/GPU RAM. The 32 MiB budget is soft because all wanted birds and ready loading fallbacks remain protected. Persistent layouts are a separate bounded cache, **24 entries / 16 MiB serialized payload**, keyed by SHA-256 of actual source/settings and validated before reuse; they contain derived species coordinates/geometry/metadata, no raw observations or distance matrices. **Clear collection, saved copy & layouts** clears both collection storage and layout caches. Current desktop/narrow-viewport timing and settlement observations are recorded separately in [README-PERFORMANCE.md](<README-PERFORMANCE.md>).

## Prioritized remaining visual work

| Priority | Improvement | Where / acceptance criterion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High | Make the plot the primary surface on phones | Collapse infrequently used settings; keep country, view and selection reachable. Test on actual iPhone and Android with real fingers, page scrolling and pinch gestures. Preserve the complete collection and accessible species list. |
| High | Define a clear selected/hovered state at very crowded overviews | Compare a subtle image-local accent with the current inset tree border and map name behavior. Avoid enlarging or moving a selected illustration over its neighbors. Verify tap, keyboard and pointer selection. |
| Medium | Explain color groups without adding another crowded label ring | Test selective inner order labels or a compact group legend linked to the picker. Use the decorative color angles, not source order sectors, and retain collision checks. |
| Medium | Reduce repetitive counts and interpretation text | The graph count, group summary and footer serve different contexts but can repeat information. Keep the in-plot count and export interpretation; compare a shorter footer and consolidated status on phones. |
| Medium | Improve the overview-to-detail transition | Consider a short, reduced-motion-aware camera transition and a stable name location. Never animate individual birds independently in a scientific view or reorder a group silently. |
| Medium | Differentiate artwork absence, loading and source-placement absence | Keep loading markers distinct from missing-image fallbacks and placement gaps. A bird may have an image but no source position, or the reverse. Test empty and partially covered groups as well as the complete personal list. |
| Medium | Give True size a clearer explanation at very large species counts | Accurate relative dimensions necessarily make some small birds tiny at a complete fit. Keep Averaged size easy to reach, show the selected bird at useful detail, and avoid a hidden minimum-size clamp that changes the stated ratios. |
| Medium | Evaluate footprint-spaced branches across more collection shapes | The preferred pure-footprint angular allocation is implemented. Compare sparse lists, shared source concepts and large true-size groups while retaining topology, source-tip sequence, dated radial distances and readable labels. Do not interpret wedge width as evolutionary distance or species richness. |
| Low | Refine typography, density and logo at small sizes | Review the toy tree at 16/32/40 px, contrast, long scientific names, text scaling and focus rings. Retain the compact vector asset and source credits. |

Future improvements should be compared using the same personal fixture, a small sample, repeated source concepts, unresolved placements and narrow/wide viewports. Visual preference is not a reason to alter the scientific source or silently omit birds.
